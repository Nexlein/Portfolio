K-Means Clustering (Unsupervised)

Unsupervised Learning means we give the algorithm raw, unlabelled data (just grey points). We don't tell it what the points are. The algorithm must discover patterns and group the data into 'K' distinct clusters entirely on its own based on spatial similarity.

How it learns:

  • Assign: Calculate the distance from each point to every centroid, assigning the point to the closest one.
  • Update: Move each centroid exactly to the center (mean) of all points assigned to it.
  • Repeat until centroids stop moving.

Parameters

3
